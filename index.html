The gap

The law is specific. The practice often isn't.

Before any asset is repossessed, the National Credit Act sets out exactly what has to happen first. In practice, these steps are the ones most often skipped.

§129

A written default notice must be sent before any legal action begins — not a phone call, not a verbal warning.

§130

You must be given a real window to cure the default or apply for debt review before enforcement proceeds.

§131

Repossession requires a valid court order — agents arriving without one is not a lawful "self-help" repossession.
